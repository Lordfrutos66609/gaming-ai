A reader, not an AI

Lootwise retrieves pages from Wikipedia and Wikibooks and displays their material here. The layout changes for game articles and guide chapters.

It does not search every website. Community pages may be incomplete or outdated, especially for patches and detailed builds. The source and revision are shown on each article.

Nothing is saved by Lootwise. Searches and article requests go directly to the source providers. Images may have separate licensing; use their image credit link.
